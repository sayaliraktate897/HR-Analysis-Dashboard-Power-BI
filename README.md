## About
An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes employee attrition to help HR understand why employees leave.

## Key metrics
- Total employees: 1,470
- Attrition count: 237 (16.1% attrition rate)
- Average monthly income: $6,503

## Visuals
- Attrition rate by department and job role
- Headcount by age group
- Attrition split (stayed vs left)
- Attrition by overtime
- Attrition by job satisfaction and work-life balance
- Average years at company vs attrition rate by job role

## Filters
Department, Gender, OverTime

## Tools used
Power BI, Power Query (data cleaning), DAX (KPI measures)

## Key insights
- attrition is higher among employees working overtime.
-  Sales roles show the highest attrition.
